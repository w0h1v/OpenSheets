# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews or
discussions, is expected to be respectful and constructive. Disagree with
ideas, not with people. Assume good faith, explain your reasoning, and keep
the conversation about the work.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, and publishing other
people's private information are not acceptable here. If you see or
experience behaviour like that, report it privately through the repository's
security advisory form or to the maintainers; reports are handled discreetly.

Maintainers may edit, remove or lock content that breaks these expectations
and may ask people who repeatedly break them to stop contributing.
